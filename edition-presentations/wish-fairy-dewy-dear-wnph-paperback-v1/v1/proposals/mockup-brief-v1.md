# Mockup Brief v1 — The Wish Fairy and Dewy Dear

**Status:** proposal workflow only — no new cover design

## Purpose

Create realistic physical-book presentation assets from the already-established recovered cover image for *The Wish Fairy and Dewy Dear*.

The recovered image is the graphic cover input. This workflow does not redesign, redraw, recolor, reinterpret, or replace that image.

## Canonical identity

- Work: `wish-fairy-and-dewy-dear`
- Publication Expression: `wish-fairy-dewy-dear:wnph-publication-e1`
- Target Manifestation: `wish-fairy-dewy-dear:wnph-paperback-v1`

## Required input

Resolve the approved/recovered cover image through the governed WNPH publication/media seam and record its immutable asset reference in the EPP artwork section when approved for this Manifestation.

Do not create a substitute cover if the image is temporarily unavailable to the mockup pipeline. The job remains blocked on the real approved image.

## Mockup task

Once the authoritative paperback physical specification is available, place the approved cover image onto a reusable paperback mockup template that provides:

- flexible cover-stock geometry;
- a separate page block;
- fore-edge, top-edge, and bottom-edge paper surfaces;
- a subtle perfect-bound spine/hinge crease;
- physically plausible cover thickness;
- contact and cast shadows;
- paper/material response over the printed image.

The image must appear printed on the cover stock rather than acting as the geometry of the book.

## Required web presentation outputs

Generate, from the same approved artwork and template job:

1. `presentation.shelfSpine`;
2. `presentation.frontCover`;
3. `presentation.threeQuarterMockup`;
4. `presentation.detailMockup`.

If approved spine/back artwork does not yet exist, do not invent it. Generate only outputs that can be produced without fabricating publication graphics, or leave those outputs pending.

## Website use

The homepage shelf consumes the generated shelf asset and may position, lean, scale, and animate it. The selected-book state consumes the approved detail or three-quarter mockup. The website does not redraw the recovered cover or synthesize a replacement physical identity.

## Stop condition

This mockup brief is complete when the real recovered cover asset has been resolved and the physical paperback specification is authoritative enough to run a real template job. Until then, no newly designed cover art should be created.
