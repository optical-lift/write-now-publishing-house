# Cover Concept v1 — Recovered Storybook

**Status:** proposal only

## Concept

The first visual direction treats *The Wish Fairy and Dewy Dear* as a recovered small storybook rather than a modern fantasy title.

The cover is intentionally quiet. It uses a warm paper field, period-aware serif typography, botanical framing, and a small emblem built from a dew drop and simplified fairy form.

The emblem is new WNPH proposal artwork. It is not represented as surviving historical cover art.

## Palette

- paper: warm ivory / parchment
- title and rule: dark moss green
- secondary type: warm brown/taupe
- dew: muted blue-green
- botanical shapes: desaturated leaf green
- accent: subdued ochre

## Hierarchy

1. title;
2. recovered-storybook character;
3. central fairy/dew emblem;
4. author;
5. WNPH identity;
6. historical note.

## Physical intent

The art is designed to look printed on flexible paper stock. The visual proposal should not be interpreted as cloth, casewrap, foil, embossing, jacket, or rigid board.
