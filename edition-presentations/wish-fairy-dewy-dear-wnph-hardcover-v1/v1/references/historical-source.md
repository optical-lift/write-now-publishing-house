# Historical Source Reference — The Wish Fairy and Dewy Dear

This file records the source evidence governing the recognizable physical character of the WNPH restored edition. It does **not** silently convert approximate historical measurements into exact WNPH manufacturing specifications.

## Library of Congress

Source item: https://www.loc.gov/item/22008427/

The Library of Congress catalogue identifies:

- title: *The Wish fairy and Dewy Dear*
- author: Alice Ross Colver
- publication: Philadelphia, Henry Altemus Company, c1922
- extent: 63, [1] pages
- illustrated with a color frontispiece and color illustrations
- physical height: 14 cm
- public-domain status in the Library of Congress collection

WNPH's governed publication Expression currently carries seven media placements: one frontispiece and six interior color plates. Their source surfaces are held by Library of Congress IIIF references in WNPH publication custody.

WNPH publication source custody also contains the whole-book source skeleton, including the first source surface at `dewy:loc:source-surface:0001` and its Library of Congress IIIF image service. That source surface is the provenance path for recovering a higher-resolution front-cover master. The raw institutional scan is source evidence, not itself the approved restored cover, because collection markings present on the scan are absent from the established WNPH restoration.

## Henry Altemus series reference

Reference: https://henryaltemus.com/series/series190.htm

The Henry Altemus reference site describes Wish Fairy Series format 190 as:

- grey cloth;
- green lettering;
- a multicolored line-drawn/book-specific image or appliqué;
- approximately 5.5 × 4.25 inches;
- a white coated dust jacket matching the book.

The site also cautions that the pictured later version of book #3 differs in color from the original series format.

## Restoration decision

WNPH intentionally uses that historical physical character as the target for the restored Manifestation `wish-fairy-dewy-dear:wnph-hardcover-v1`.

That decision admits the following restoration direction:

- cloth hardcover / cloth case rather than paperback wrap;
- gray cloth character;
- green lettering/art treatment;
- the established recovered book-specific front image;
- historical dust-jacket character as a future governed layer when jacket artwork and construction facts are available.

It does **not** by itself establish exact contemporary printer values.

## How this evidence may be used

Allowed:

- govern the restored edition's recognizable binding and material character;
- understand historical proportions and material relationships;
- preserve the relationship of green lettering, cloth, and the book-specific image;
- recover a higher-resolution version of the already-established cover from governed source custody without redesigning it;
- inform an explicit WNPH trim/material decision when that decision is later recorded as publication truth.

Not allowed:

- copy the approximate 5.5 × 4.25 inch dimensions into the EPP as exact WNPH printer trim without an explicit manufacturing decision;
- infer WNPH spine width, board thickness, stocks, or final page block from the historical copy;
- promote a raw institutional scan to approved restored artwork merely because it is high resolution;
- invent missing spine, back, or dust-jacket graphics;
- redraw, recolor, or generatively replace the established recovered cover.
