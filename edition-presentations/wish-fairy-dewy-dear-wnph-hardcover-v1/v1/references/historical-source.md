# Historical Source Reference — The Wish Fairy and Dewy Dear

This file records source evidence for the WNPH restoration's physical design target. It does not invent manufacturing facts that the sources do not establish.

## Library of Congress

Source item: https://www.loc.gov/item/22008427/

The Library of Congress catalogue identifies:

- title: *The Wish fairy and Dewy Dear*;
- author: Alice Ross Colver;
- publication: Philadelphia, Henry Altemus Company, c1922;
- extent: 63, [1] pages;
- illustrated with a color frontispiece and color illustrations;
- physical height: 14 cm;
- public-domain status in the Library of Congress collection.

WNPH's governed publication Expression currently carries seven media placements: one frontispiece and six interior color plates. Their source surfaces are held by Library of Congress IIIF references in WNPH publication custody.

## Henry Altemus series reference

Reference: https://henryaltemus.com/series/series190.htm

The Henry Altemus reference site describes Wish Fairy Series format 190 as:

- gray cloth;
- green lettering;
- a multicolored line-drawn/book-specific image or appliqué;
- approximately 5.5 × 4.25 inches;
- a white coated dust jacket matching the book.

The site also cautions that the pictured later version of book #3 differs in color from the original series format.

## WNPH restoration decision

WNPH is intentionally restoring the physical character of the historical hardbound edition rather than converting the work to an arbitrary modern paperback.

The WNPH restored Manifestation therefore adopts:

- hardcover binding;
- cloth-case construction for the current governed package;
- gray-cloth and green-lettering/art character;
- the historical approximately 5.5 × 4.25 inch bound-book size as proportion/reference evidence only, not as an exact WNPH trim specification.

The historically evidenced matching dust jacket remains part of the restoration target, but it is not yet promoted to active EPP construction because no governed jacket spread or complete jacket specification has been approved.

## What remains unresolved

This evidence does not by itself establish:

- final WNPH trim width and height;
- WNPH final print page count;
- spine width;
- exact cloth, board, page, or jacket stock;
- exact historical production technique for the green lettering/art treatment;
- complete spine/back/jacket artwork;
- final mockup lighting or website presentation assets.

Those facts must enter through their owning publication/presentation processes rather than being inferred in frontend code.
