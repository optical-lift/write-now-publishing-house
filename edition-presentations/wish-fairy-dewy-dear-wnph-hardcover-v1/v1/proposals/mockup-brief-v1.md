# Mockup Brief v1 — The Wish Fairy and Dewy Dear

**Status:** proposal workflow only — no new cover design

## Purpose

Create realistic physical-book presentation assets for the WNPH restored edition using the already-established recovered cover artwork and the historically grounded cloth-hardcover construction.

The recovered image is the graphic cover input. This workflow does not redesign, redraw, recolor, reinterpret, or replace that image.

- Work: `wish-fairy-and-dewy-dear`
- Publication Expression: `wish-fairy-dewy-dear:wnph-publication-e1`
- Target Manifestation: `wish-fairy-dewy-dear:wnph-hardcover-v1`
- Manifestation ID: `0bb1920c-3df4-4ae9-9e4a-67ba8b39ff82`

## Governed restoration target

Use the Henry Altemus c1922 physical edition as the restoration target now intentionally adopted for the WNPH physical edition:

- historical bound-book size approximately 4.25 × 5.5 inches, used as a proportion reference rather than an exact WNPH trim specification;
- gray cloth case;
- green lettering/art treatment;
- book-specific multicolor cover image/appliqué character;
- matching white coated dust-jacket character, pending approved jacket artwork/specification.

The active v1 mockup construction is `cloth-case`. Do not fabricate final trim, jacket spread, spine width, stock, or back/spine graphics merely to complete the render.

## Required artwork input

Resolve the highest-resolution approved/recovered cover image and record its immutable asset reference in the EPP artwork section when approved. The current 320 × 480 repository derivative may be used only as a temporary interaction reference and must not become the final detail-presentation master.

## Mockup task

Once the remaining required physical inputs exist, apply the approved artwork to a reusable cloth-hardcover template that provides:

- rigid board geometry and historically appropriate proportions;
- gray-cloth material response;
- recessed page block;
- hinge/joint channels;
- board edges and restrained overhang;
- contact and cast shadows;
- approved green case lettering/art treatment only where source/production rules support it.

The artwork must appear attached to or printed/stamped on the physical case according to the approved restoration specification rather than acting as the book geometry itself.

## Required web presentation outputs

Generate from the same governed job when the required inputs support them:

1. `presentation.shelfSpine`;
2. `presentation.frontCover`;
3. `presentation.threeQuarterMockup`;
4. `presentation.detailMockup`.

Add `presentation.jacketOffMockup` or jacketed outputs only after the jacket itself is governed.

## Website use

The homepage shelf may position, lean, scale, and animate the resulting presentation assets. Until they exist, a lightweight interaction fallback may approximate geometry but must remain clearly provisional and must not become publication authority.

## Stop condition

This brief is complete when the high-resolution recovered artwork and authoritative physical inputs, including final WNPH manufacturing geometry, are sufficient to run a real cloth-hardcover template job. Unknown manufacturing facts remain unknown rather than being inferred by the website.
